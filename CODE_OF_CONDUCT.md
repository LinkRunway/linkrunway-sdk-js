# Code of conduct

LinkRunway welcomes participation regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and specific. Critique the work rather than the person.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.
- Respect privacy, consent, and different perspectives. Share only information you are entitled to disclose.
- Keep discussions relevant and give others room to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **support@linkrunway.com** with the subject **Community conduct**. Include links or redacted evidence and describe what happened. Do not post private reports publicly or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

Maintainers review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical, and consider context and severity. Responses may include a request to change behavior, content removal, a warning, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers must follow the same standards. If you believe a moderation decision was mistaken, explain the concern through the same private contact.
